### 📊 Key Research Findings

<div align="center">

| Metric | Clean Data | 5% Mislabeled | 20% Mislabeled |
|--------|------------|---------------|----------------|
| **CNN Accuracy** | 91% ✅ | 83% ⚠️ | 73% ❌ |
| **ANN Accuracy** | 84% ✅ | 79% ⚠️ | 67% ❌ |

</div>

</div>

> 💡 **Key Insight**: Just 5% mislabeled data reduced CNN accuracy by 8%! This proves that **data quality is more critical than data quantity** in machine learning.
